Dear Rashi,

Happy birthday, you. ❤️

It's still funny to me that this all began because my seating got changed. I wasn't even planning to talk to you. And now you're the person I could talk to for hours and still have a hundred things left to say.

I keep our first date in Barsana close to my heart. That Activa ride, you sitting beside me, your hand in mine coming over my shoulder — I wish I could revisit that little moment whenever I wanted.

Then came our casual highway dates, the hugs that make everything feel okay, and those kisses. The accidental one, the awkward almost-kiss that made us embarrassed, and then the one that finally happened. Somehow, even the clumsy bits became my favourite memories.

"They say 11:11 is when you're supposed to make a wish. Honestly, I never really believed in angel numbers, wishing on clocks, or waiting on falling stars. But looking at you... for you, I will believe in it. You are my 11:11 — the wish I didn't even know my heart was making, and somehow the best thing that ever came true."

I don't need every day to be extraordinary. I just want more ordinary days with you in them. More conversations, more rides, more laughter, more hugs, and more stories that belong only to us.

I love you, Rashi. I hope this year is gentle with you, full of beautiful surprises, and gives you a thousand reasons to smile. I hope I get to be beside you for many of them.

Happy birthday, my 11:11. My favourite plot twist.
